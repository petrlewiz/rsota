Complete the application below to enroll in the program that fits your calling. Once it's reviewed and approved, you'll receive next steps for beginning your studies.

Program Costs

Please review the following fees and payment information before completing your application.

  • Registration Fee: $50 per program
  • Workbook Fee: $25 per workbook
  • Course Fee: $100 per course
  • Graduation Fee: $110

Students are responsible for purchasing all required textbooks and workbooks.

All applicable program, workbook, and course fees are due after the application has been reviewed and approved. The graduation fee is payable separately at the time of graduation.
