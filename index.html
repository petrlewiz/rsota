Our Foundation

Begin with the Word,
grow in truth

Founded in 1999 by Rev. Dr. Everald Lewis, Rhema Studies of Theology Association & Biblical Training (RSOTA) is committed to providing Christ-centered, doctrinally sound biblical education.

Students can expect systematic study of Scripture, theology, and practical ministry, equipping them with the knowledge, understanding, and tools needed to faithfully teach the Word and effectively serve in ministry.

Why RSOTA?

With a variety of programs to choose from, RSOTA provides students with opportunities to grow in biblical understanding, develop practical ministry skills, and prepare for effective service in the work of the Lord.
