Our Foundation

Formed by Scripture, prepared for ministry

RSOTA began as a small gathering of pastors meeting to study the biblical languages together. That same posture — patient, communal, text-first — still shapes every course we teach today.

Students move through a sequence built on three convictions: that sound doctrine grows out of careful exegesis, that theology is meant to be lived in a congregation, and that teaching Scripture well is itself an act of discipleship.

Study Format

Learn where you are

Every program runs both on our campus and at a distance, with the same faculty and coursework either way.

On Campus

  • Weekday classes with full faculty access
  • Weekly chapel and mentorship groups
  • On-site library and language lab
  • Supervised local church placements

Distance Learning

  • Live seminars streamed weekly
  • Recorded lectures, available anytime
  • Reading cohorts by region and time zone
  • Two campus intensives per year
