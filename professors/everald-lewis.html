Dr. Everald Lewis
Founder & President
Bio

Bishop, Dr. Everald Lewis has been in the ministry for over thirty years and has been used by God in various ways in His service. He is dedicated in his walk and service for the Lord. His passion and deep commitment to teach the Word of God is unflinching. Dr. Lewis is the Founder and President of Rhema Studies of Theology Association. He is also the senior pastor of Faith and Power International Ministries church. He was a professor at Canada Christian College.
Dr. Lewis is an ordained minister and is the author of many books such as, "Becoming a True Christian", "Understanding the Will of God" and "The General Epistle". He also wrote outline studies on such books as Ephesians, the Tabernacle, the Minor Prophet, the Book of Romans, and Galatians.
Dr. Lewis is an excellent conference and seminar speaker. God used him in countries such as, USA, England, Trinidad, India and Jamaica. Only heaven will record the blessings he has been to the body of Christ, and continues to be, by God's enabling grace and power.
