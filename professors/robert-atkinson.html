Rev. Robert Atkinson

Rev. Robert Atkinson is the founding pastor of Puritan Evangelical. He is a husband, a father, and a son. He is committed to the preaching of the gospel. He previously made several appearances on the popular T.V program “Night Light” a Huntley Street Production. He presently serves on the teaching staff of RSOTA. He holds a Bachelor of Arts degree (Theology) from California State Christian University and a Master of Theology (M.Th. ) from The Mount Olive Bible Institute and Seminary.
