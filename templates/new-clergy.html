Application

Clergy Credential Application

Complete the application below to apply for clergy credentials. Once it's reviewed and approved, you'll receive next steps regarding ordination.

Required Supporting Documents

In addition to this application, please prepare and submit the following documents separately.

  • A written statement describing your Conversion (on separate paper).
  • A written statement regarding your Call to the Ministry (on separate paper).
  • Three (3) Reference Letters: one from your Senior Pastor, and two from Ordained Ministers.

Please email all supporting documents to dr.lewis@rsota.org.

Application Fees
  • Application Fee: $100.00 — due with this application (non-refundable)
  • Two-Year Fee: $180.00 — payable before the Ordination Service

Please e-transfer the $100.00 application fee to dr.lewis@rsota.ca. Please note, this fee is non-refundable.

The $180.00 two-year fee is payable before the Ordination Service and may also be e-transferred to dr.lewis@rsota.ca.
